Here the target is an image, but the candidate is a bounded drawing program: a sequence of simple shapes, colors, and opacity choices. The GPU evaluates possible next layers without materializing a full candidate-by-pixel matrix.

The left canvas is the target; the right canvas is the result from the recorded search.
